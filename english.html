About the Book

Water and Xenoglossia is a book based on personal experience, many years of observations, reflections and questions by the author.

The author explores possible connections between water, memory, information, the human body and a phenomenon he calls xenoglossia.

This work does not claim to be scientific proof. It is an attempt to share personal observations and raise questions that the author considers important for further thought and investigation.

“Knowledge that a person receives should be available to others, so that everyone can think, observe and ask questions for themselves.”

About the Author

Hayk Samvelovich Kharatyan is an author, engineer and independent researcher who has spent many years observing and recording his own ideas, experiences and reflections concerning water and human consciousness.

In this book, he presents a personal perspective on questions that developed through many years of observation and reflection.

Important Note

The material presented in this book reflects the author's personal experience, observations, assumptions and questions. It is not presented as a scientific study or as scientific proof.

This book does not replace scientific research or professional medical, psychological or other expert advice.

The purpose of this publication is to share ideas and personal observations freely with readers around the world.
